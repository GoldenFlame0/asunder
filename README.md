# ASUNDER

Asunder is a modpack developed largely by GoldenFlameZero. It's a modpack with a focus on adventure, with a strong tech backbone as you unravel the world (on a scale of Terraria to Factorio, it's on the Terraria side).

## Guiding Principles

These are the principles for how the pack is put together, and how progression is intended to feed into.

*1. The World is Old*

The apocalypse has been and gone. Ruins of the past and remnants of their successors dot the landscape. Nobody knows how the world ended, and it was so long ago that it doesn't really matter anymore. You're just another creature surviving in the ruins of people who knew more than you.

*2. The World is Alive*

Grass grows, animals reproduce. Iron rusts. Stone gets covered with moss. Hordes of zombies attack. The moon changes and brings dark events. You can't just build and sit contently. You have to build defences and fortifications. You have to adapt and react to make the most of it.

*3. You Are Smart*

What sets the player apart is their ability to scavenge and invent. Only you can delve deep into magic and technology, to harness what was lost and what has yet to be. You can cross dimensions, find apocalyptic logs, fit together pieces of the past and the shattered worlds of reality.
